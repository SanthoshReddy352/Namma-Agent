## What They Do

Sripa Aqua Marine Private Limited is engaged in **shrimp (prawn) seed production and supply**. Their core business includes:

- Manufacturing, exporting, supplying, and retailing of **Prawn Seed (Shrimp Seed)**
- Primary product: **Vannamei Shrimp Seeds** (Litopenaeus vannamei)
- The company operates a **CAA (Coastal Aquaculture Authority) approved hatchery** authorized for the import of **SPF (Specific Pathogen Free) broodstock** and seed production
- Historically also involved in **Black Tiger Shrimp (Penaeus monodon)** seed production

The company has been involved in shrimp seed production since at least **1996**, as recorded in the DCMSME Nellore district industrial profile.

- **Import Authorized:** Permitted to import Specific Pathogen Free (SPF) broodstock from international suppliers
- **MPEDA Listed:** Appears in MPEDA (Marine Products Export Development Authority) publications related to seafood exports

- Consistently appears in CAA's lists of registered hatcheries permitted for consortium operations and broodstock import

## What Makes Them Unique

- **Long-standing presence:** Incorporated in 1994, the company has been operating for over 32 years, making it one of the older shrimp hatcheries in India
- **CAA-approved import status:** Not all hatcheries have CAA approval for direct import of SPF broodstock — Sripa Aqua Marine holds this authorization, allowing them to source high-quality specific-pathogen-free broodstock directly from international suppliers
- **Strategic location:** Based in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, which is a major hub for aquaculture in India, particularly known for shrimp farming and hatchery operations
- **Direct broodstock supply chain:** The company has been consistently importing SPF L. vannamei broodstock from international sources (as documented in CAA import lists from 2015 to 2024)
